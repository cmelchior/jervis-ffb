# 2025 Sevens

This page documents any ambiguities and decisions made by Jervis when 
implementing the Sevens rules the 2025 ruleset. They are described in Spike 22.

## Page 9 - Apothecary  

In the 2020 variant, Apothecaries only worked on 4+, rather than re-rolling
the Casuality roll. In Spike 22, this description is gone, which means that
the only behavior described in BB20205 is the one found in the rulebook.

However, that behavior isn't useful for Sevens as there is no Casualty table.

Since that leaves the behavior undefined. Jervis has opted for re-using the 
2020 behavior for now.

## Page 10 - The Kick-off

In Sevens, deviating the kick-off now uses 2D6 for distance, choosing the 
lowest roll. This makes it unclear how the Kick skill is used in combination.

Since Jervis already opted to follow the NAF interpretation, which means that
we can choose to use Kick after the roll, Jervis has opted to apply the same
interpretation here. 

This means that in Jervis, Kick applies to the lowest of the two D6 rolls, and 
the choice is made after both dice are rolled.
